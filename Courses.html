Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a course take?

Most courses are designed to be completed in 6-12 weeks if you practice for 15-20 minutes daily. However, you can learn at your own pace.

Do I need prior experience?

Not at all! Our beginner courses assume zero prior knowledge. We also have placement tests if you have studied the language before.

Are the certificates internationally recognized?

Yes, our advanced certificates are aligned with CEFR standards and are accepted by many global institutions and employers.
